# Privacy Policy – Stack Tower

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Stack Tower.

This Privacy Policy explains how Stack Tower ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our mobile game.

Stack Tower is a casual mobile game. The core gameplay is designed to work offline. However, certain features and services use third-party services, including Google Play Games Services, Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics.

By using the App, you acknowledge the data practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App and which services are enabled, the following categories of information may be collected or processed.

### A. Google Play Games Services

If you use Google Play Games features, Google may process information such as:

- Gamer ID or player identifier.
- Gamer name and profile information, such as avatar.
- Achievement progress and unlocked achievements.
- Game scores and leaderboard information.
- Information necessary to authenticate and provide game-related services.
- Device and diagnostic information used to operate and improve the service.

We use these services to provide achievements, leaderboards, and related gaming functionality.

Google Play Games sign-in is optional where supported. Core gameplay does not require a Google Play Games account.

### B. Advertising – Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

The advertising SDK may automatically collect or process:

- IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
- Advertising ID and other device or app identifiers.
- Device information, such as device model and operating system.
- Advertising interactions, including ad impressions, clicks, and video views.
- App interaction information.
- Diagnostic and performance information.

This information may be used for advertising delivery, ad measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and advertising-related services.

Depending on your region, applicable consent requirements, and your device settings, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

### C. Firebase Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the game and to improve its functionality and experience.

Information may include:

- App launches and usage activity.
- Gameplay and feature interaction events.
- Session information.
- Device information and operating system details.
- App instance identifiers.
- General usage and diagnostic information.

We use this information to understand game usage, identify areas for improvement, and improve the overall user experience.

### D. Firebase Crashlytics

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify and investigate crashes and stability problems.

Information may include:

- Crash reports and stack traces.
- App version and operating system information.
- Device model and technical information.
- Crash timestamps and application state information.
- App instance identifiers and related diagnostic information.

We use this information to identify technical problems and improve app stability.

## 3. Locally Stored Game Information

Some game information, such as gameplay progress, personal best scores, and unlocked content, may be stored locally on your device.

This local information supports offline gameplay.

When supported and enabled, certain game progress or achievements may be synchronized with Google Play Games Services.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data may remove locally stored game progress. Information already synchronized with third-party services may remain subject to those services' retention and deletion policies.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information processed through the App and its integrated services for the following purposes:

- Providing core game functionality.
- Providing achievements and leaderboard features.
- Displaying and measuring advertisements.
- Understanding how users interact with the App.
- Monitoring crashes and improving stability.
- Diagnosing technical issues.
- Preventing fraudulent or abusive activity.
- Maintaining and improving the App.

We do not use this information for purposes unrelated to the App and its disclosed services.

## 5. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell users' personal information.

However, certain information may be transmitted to or processed by third-party service providers when their services are used.

These providers include:

**Google Play Games Services:** Used for gaming identity, achievements, leaderboard functionality, and related services.

**Google AdMob:** Used for advertising delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention.

**Google Firebase Analytics:** Used for app usage analytics and product improvement.

**Google Firebase Crashlytics:** Used for crash reporting, diagnostics, and app stability improvements.

These services are operated by Google LLC and/or its affiliates. Their processing of information is also governed by Google's applicable privacy terms and policies.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or valid legal process.

## 6. Advertising and Consent

The App uses advertising services that may process device identifiers and other information for advertising purposes.

Where required by applicable law, we will request the necessary consent before enabling relevant advertising-related data processing.

You may also have options to manage advertising preferences through your device settings or available privacy controls.

Your advertising experience may vary depending on your location, consent choices, and device settings.

## 7. Data Security

We and our third-party service providers use appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect information.

Information transmitted by supported Google services is generally protected using encryption in transit.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted by you, removed through the App's available functionality, or removed when the App's data is cleared or the App is uninstalled.

Information processed by Google Play Games Services, AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics is retained according to the respective service providers' retention policies and configuration.

You can manage your Google account and Google Play Games profile information through Google's account and Play Games settings.

You can also reset or manage your advertising identifier through your Android device settings, where supported.

For privacy-related requests concerning information handled by us, contact us using the email address provided in this policy.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Stack Tower is a general-audience casual game and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Third-party services integrated into the App may have their own age restrictions and data-handling requirements. Advertising and other services are subject to applicable age-related requirements and consent rules.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter.
